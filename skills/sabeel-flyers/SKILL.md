# Sabeel Institute flyers

Every Sabeel program (a class, series, camp, or gathering) is announced with a
flyer. The same image is printed, shared on WhatsApp and Instagram, and shown on
the program's page of the website, so it has to work on a wall and on a phone.
This skill covers what a flyer says and how it is laid out. Colours and the
logo come from the **sabeel-color-scheme** skill; read it too.

The latest version of this skill is in
[Sabeel-Institute/brand](https://github.com/Sabeel-Institute/brand) under
`skills/sabeel-flyers/`.

## Facts come from the organisation

Never invent a date, time, fee, name, venue, or link. When one is missing,
leave a marked placeholder such as `[FEE?]` and ask. Copy names, fees, and
program titles exactly as the organisation gives them.

## What every flyer says

In this order of prominence, top to bottom:

1. **Logo.** The Sabeel logo at the top, usually centred. When a partner
   co-hosts, their logo sits beside it at the same visual height.
2. **Title.** The program's name, as the largest text on the flyer. A short
   tagline or subtitle may follow.
3. **Who it is for.** For example "Sisters only", "Adult women", or "For teens
   13–16 · Boys & girls".
4. **When.** The start date, the day or days, and the time range with the time
   zone. For a series, add the number of sessions or the end date.
5. **Where.** In person, online, or both, with the venue ("Masjid Istiqlal",
   "Online via Zoom"). Give the street address for events open to the public.
6. **Who teaches.** Each instructor, with the honorific the organisation uses
   (see Writing).
7. **Cost and deadline.** The fee, or "Free", and the registration deadline if
   there is one.
8. **How to register.** "Register now" with a QR code for the registration link,
   and **oursabeel.com** at the bottom.

Optional extras: three to five short lines on what students will learn, a note
such as a gift for early registration, or a short quote.

## Design

Each program has its own theme: an illustration or photograph, a season, a
mood. Recent flyers vary widely in colour, and that is fine. The brand stays
constant through:

- **The logo:** the main version on light backgrounds, the reverse version only
  on dark ones, never recoloured (see sabeel-color-scheme).
- **The information blocks:** the same content, in the order above.
- **Readable type and contrast.**

When a flyer has no strong theme of its own, use the brand palette: a warm
ivory background, a raspberry title, gold dividers and accents, and text in the
deep brown `#3A2F28`. Taupe and brand-strength gold are too faint for small
text.

- **Readable on a phone.** The date, time, and place must be readable when the
  flyer fills a phone screen, about 390 px wide. On a flyer 1080 px wide, set
  those lines at 30 px or larger. Keep decorative script lettering to the
  title.
- **Text over pictures** sits on a solid or softened panel, never directly on
  busy detail.
- **Icons** for the when and where lines (calendar, clock, map pin) make them
  quicker to scan. Use one icon style and one colour.
- **QR code.**
  - Make it dark on light, with a clear margin around it.
  - Print it at least 2 cm (0.8 in) across, which is about a tenth of a Letter
    flyer's width, and larger on a poster read from a distance.
  - Label it "Scan to register", and scan it yourself before sharing.
  - Someone viewing the flyer on their own phone can't scan it, so also give
    the link or oursabeel.com as text.

## Formats

- **US Letter, portrait** (8.5 × 11 in; 2550 × 3300 px at 300 dpi). Most Sabeel
  flyers use this size.
- **Square** (1080 × 1080 px) or **4:5** (1080 × 1350 px) for Instagram and
  WhatsApp.
- Keep text and the QR code at least 5% in from every edge, so nothing is lost
  when the flyer is printed or cropped.
- Export a PDF for printing and a PNG or high-quality JPG for sharing.

## Writing

- **Times** carry the zone: `10:00 AM–1:00 PM CT`. Write CT, not CST: Houston
  is on daylight time (CDT) from March to November, so CST is wrong for most of
  the year.
- **Ranges** use an en dash: unspaced between times (`7:00–8:00 PM`), spaced
  between dates (`Sept 14 – Oct 26`).
- **Dates** name the weekday where it helps ("Starting Monday, September 14").
  Pick one style per flyer and keep to it.
- **Names and honorifics** appear the same way on every flyer and on the
  website: Ustadhah for Sameera Shah, Ust. for the other teachers, and Sr. and
  Br. as the organisation uses them. Spell each name as the organisation does.
- **‘Alimiyyah** takes the opening mark. **Qur’an** takes the curly apostrophe
  in running text; a program's name keeps the spelling it was given.
- **Quotes and apostrophes** are curly (’ “ ”).
- **Case:** use one case style for headings throughout a flyer. Keep capitals
  for short labels, not for sentences.
- Write the web address as **oursabeel.com**, without "www".

## When the flyer is final

The website shows each program's flyer on its page and repeats the flyer's
facts. They live in the program's folder in the website repository,
[Sabeel-Institute/sabeel-website-test](https://github.com/Sabeel-Institute/sabeel-website-test),
whose AGENTS.md lists the fields. The flyer and the page must agree: when a
date, time, fee, or link changes, change both.
